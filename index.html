Facility energy planner

Pick the customer's supply voltage and tariff, set the facility size, then compare four ways of running one day. Load profiles are synthetic or from a case study. Check every rate against the customer's own TNB bill.

Customer tariff (TNB non-domestic)

The tariff follows the supply voltage on the bill, not the size alone. Small shops and offices are usually LV, factories, malls and campuses MV, very large sites HV.
