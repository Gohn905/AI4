    What the optimiser decides

    For every hour of the day, the optimiser chooses all of these together, so it can plan the battery and the equipment around the highest billed hour.

    • Battery: when to charge, when to discharge, and how full it is. It starts the day half full and must end at least half full. Round-trip efficiency is 90%.
    • Shiftable equipment: how much of the flexible load runs in each hour, only between 09:00 and 17:00, with total energy unchanged.
    • Grid: how much to import each hour, within the grid limit, with the highest billed hour as the maximum demand.
    • Solar: all of it is used on site first. No export is assumed.

    Goal: lowest bill (hourly energy charge plus maximum demand charge). Balanced also adds a carbon price and a battery wear cost. Production-first moves no equipment.

    Rates follow the TNB RP4 schedule (1 Jul 2025 to 31 Dec 2027) and are fixed here. AFA changes every month, so it stays editable. The monthly retail charge is the same under every strategy, so it does not change the savings and is left out.

    Not included yet: diesel generator or gas, individual machines with start and stop rules, real meter history for the demand forecast (it is trained on synthetic history for now), export income, forecast uncertainty, and the month-long demand record.
