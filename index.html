Strategy

What the optimiser decides

For every hour of the day, the optimiser chooses all of these together, so it can plan the battery and the equipment around the highest billed hour.

  • Battery: when to charge, when to discharge, and how full it is. It starts the day half full and must end at least half full. Round-trip efficiency is 90%.
  • Shiftable equipment: how much of the flexible load runs in each hour, only between 09:00 and 17:00, with total energy unchanged.
  • Grid: how much to import each hour, within the grid limit, with the highest billed hour as the maximum demand.
  • Solar: all of it is used on site first. No export is assumed.

Goal: lowest bill (hourly energy charge plus maximum demand charge). The baseline is solar only. Cost-saving minimises the bill. Balanced moves half as much equipment and adds a carbon price (RM 0.10/kg) and a small battery wear cost. Production-first moves no equipment at all. These weights are placeholders to tune with the customer.

Not included yet: diesel or gas, individual machines with start and stop rules, real meter history (the demand forecast is trained on synthetic history), export income, forecast uncertainty, the retail charge, the 1.6% KWTBB levy and tax.
